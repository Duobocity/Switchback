        How the score and the optimizer work

        Each route is resampled every 20 m and the radius of every bend is measured, the method the Curvature project (roadcurvature.com) uses to rank roads. Corners near turns and intersections don't count, so a city grid doesn't look twisty.

        • Curves. Share of the drive in corners tighter than 175 m radius. Full marks at 35%.
        • Open road. Share of the drive off interstates and out of town.
        • Hills. Feet climbed per mile. Full marks at 80 ft/mi.
        • Traffic. Live delay as a share of drive time. Zero at 25% delay.
        • Flow. Turns and junctions per 10 miles. Fewer means fewer stops.

        The fun score is the average of those five, weighted by your sliders. Maximize fun tries every Auto setting, then tries detours off to each side (A to B) or loops heading out in eight directions (Loop), and keeps the best route inside your time limit.

        Road ratings use Curvature's scale: 300+ has a few good corners, 1,000+ is a fun road, 3,000+ is worth a trip.
